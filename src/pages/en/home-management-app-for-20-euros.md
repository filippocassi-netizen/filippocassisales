For 20 euros of Claude API and infrastructure, my partner and I built the app that runs our home.

We both use it every day: calendars, appointments, the shopping list and shared expenses.

No app did exactly what the two of us needed.

So we made our own.

I built it with Claude Code.

But the thing that stayed with me is those 20 euros.

I don't count them as a cost. I count them as the price of a course.

A few months ago I couldn't build anything. I learned, I tried, I got things wrong.

Now, when I need a tool and can't find it, I ask myself:

**"Can I just build it myself?"**

And I think it's the same for a lot of small businesses.

A shop, a restaurant, a tradesperson: they might lose half an hour every day on something a small piece of software could fix.

Building it used to not be worth it.

Now, maybe, it is.

I'm giving it a go.

For now the only place it really runs is my house.
