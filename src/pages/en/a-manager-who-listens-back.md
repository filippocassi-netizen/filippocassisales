I built myself a manager who has the time to listen back to me.

Not because I don't have one. Because nobody, anywhere, has the time to re-listen to everyone's calls.

Here's how it works. I take the transcript of one of my calls and paste it in. The system reads it back to me, checks the points I work through in a negotiation (pain, impact, why now, cost of inaction, goal) and tells me where I was weak. Then it writes the call summary to send to the company.

Building it took me a few hours. I'm not a programmer, but with Claude Code the technical part moves fast. Most of my time went into waiting for the model to answer.

Then I opened the first report. And I got angry.

It was right. That's why it bothered me. Nobody had ever told me like that, without dancing around it.

At 25 I would have defended myself: that call was an exception, that client was different. At 34 I read the whole thing and on the next call I changed what needed changing.

The tool does the easy part: it reads and it flags. The hard part is listening to it instead of defending yourself, and that part stayed mine.

Now every call I make has someone reviewing it. Even when it's a bad day and I'd rather not know.
